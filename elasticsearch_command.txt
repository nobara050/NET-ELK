Theo dõi cluster
GET /_cluster/health

Kiểm tra tài nguyên
GET /_cat/nodes?v&h=name,ip,node.role,master,heap.percent,ram.percent,cpu,uptime

Theo dõi index
GET /_cat/indices?v&s=index

Bỏ qua index hệ thống
GET /_cat/indices/*,-.*?v&s=index

Theo dõi shard
GET /_cat/shards?v
